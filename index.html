Roof restoration Brisbane

Tired roof to street's best in five steps.

A proper restoration adds years to your roof for a fraction of the cost of a new one. Here's exactly what happens up there.

01
STEP 01

Roof health check

We get up there and check every tile, ridge cap, valley and flashing. You get photos, straight answers and a fixed quote. No surprises.

STEP 02

High pressure clean

Years of moss, lichen and grime get blasted off. That clean patch in the photo is what the whole roof looks like when we're done.

STEP 03

Repair, rebed and repoint

Broken tiles swapped out. Ridge caps rebedded and repointed with flexible pointing so they stay put when the wind picks up.

STEP 04

Prime and seal

A primer and sealer coat locks everything down and gives the colour something to grip. Skip this and the finish won't last.

STEP 05

Two colour coats

Quality roof membrane in your pick of colour. The result looks brand new and it's built for the Queensland sun.

Quick answers
How much does a roof restoration cost in Brisbane?

It depends on the size and pitch of your roof, the condition of the tiles and how much repair work is needed first. We inspect it and give you a fixed written quote for free.

Can you come out for an emergency leak?

Yes. Call 0449 168 985 any time. We'll make the roof safe and watertight first, then sort the permanent repair.

Should I repair or restore?

If the roof is sound and the problem is local, a repair does the job. If tiles are faded, pointing is crumbling and moss has moved in, a restoration is better value. We'll tell you honestly which one you need.

How long does a restoration take?

Most homes take a few days, depending on the weather and the amount of repair work. We'll give you a clear timeframe with your quote.

Do you work on metal roofs too?

Yes. Tile, metal, corrugated iron, slate, flat roofs and more.
